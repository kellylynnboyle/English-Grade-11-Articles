# Weekly Article Topic Preferences

Standing guidance for anyone (or any automated run) picking the news article
for this week's annotation assignment. Read this before selecting a topic.

## Student

16 years old, Grade 11. Current classes: English 11, Physics, Algebra 2,
Sign Language, Animation.

## Topic rules

- Must satisfy the assignment's own rule: current world or local news, NOT a
  celebrity fluff piece or sports article (see assignment instructions).
- Prefer articles that connect to one of the student's classes:
  - **Animation** — animation/film industry news, studio releases, animation
    technology, animators/labor, awards.
  - **Physics** — physics research, space, energy, engineering breakthroughs.
  - **Algebra 2 / math** — mathematics discoveries, competitions, math in the
    news (elections, cryptography, data), math education.
  - **Sign Language** — deaf community news, ASL/sign language recognition,
    accessibility, disability rights.
- Rotate across these four areas week to week — don't repeat the same class's
  subject area two weeks in a row.
- Keep it age-appropriate for a 16-year-old: avoid graphic violence or content
  that needs heavy content warnings. Serious/substantive is fine; gratuitous
  detail is not.
- Each week, present 3-5 options across different classes and let the
  student (or parent) pick before drafting the full annotation.

## Where this is used

This file is the source of truth for topic selection on this recurring
assignment. Update it directly if the preferences change (e.g. new classes
next semester).
